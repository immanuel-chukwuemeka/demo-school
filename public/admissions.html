How to Apply

Six Simple Steps to Enrolment

From enquiry to resumption, we make the process smooth and transparent.

  • 1. Enquire & Tour

    Call, WhatsApp or email us. Book a campus tour to see our facilities and meet the staff.

  • 2. Submit Application

    Fill the application form online or at the school office with the child's birth certificate and passport photograph.

  • 3. Assessment / Placement Test

    Early years children attend an observation session. Primary and Secondary entrants take an entrance assessment in numeracy and literacy.

  • 4. Admission Offer

    Successful candidates receive an admission letter along with the acceptance terms and fee schedule.

  • 5. Acceptance & Payment

    Return the signed acceptance form and pay the tuition acceptance fee to secure the place.

  • 6. Resumption

    Attend resumption, collect uniforms and books, and join our community.

Entry Requirements

What You'll Need

Creche & Nursery (Ages 1–5)

  • Birth certificate or baptismal certificate
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Completed health & immunization records
  • Parent/guardian ID for the school file

Primary & Secondary (Ages 5–16+)

  • Birth certificate and previous report cards (2 terms)
  • Transfer certificate / testimonial for above Primary 1
  • Passport photographs (4)
  • Entrance assessment result (scheduled by the school)

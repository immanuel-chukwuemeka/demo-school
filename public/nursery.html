Where Play Is the Work of Learning

Our nursery blends Montessori philosophy with the Nigerian early years curriculum. Children learn phonics, early numeracy, fine motor skills and — most importantly — how to share, ask questions and try again.

  • Phonics & pre-reading — Jolly Phonics and daily book corners.
  • Number sense — counting, sorting, patterns and shapes.
  • Creative arts — music, art, drama and cultural songs.
  • Social & emotional — manners, routines and independence.
